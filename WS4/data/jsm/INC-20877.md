## Description

PostgreSQL stopped at 23:14 with "could not write to file pg_wal/xlogtemp: No space left on device" and restarted at 23:21.

## Root cause

/srv/wal on bkp-01 was not mounted after the reboot of 4 Oct. Every WAL archive attempt from db-prd-02 failed from 5 Oct 01:02 (rsync: Permission denied), and pg_wal grew until /var/lib/postgresql was full. The CRITICAL alert (95%) at 03:12 on 6 Oct was not acted on.

## Resolution

/srv/wal mounted on bkp-01 at 23:17 and the fstab entry corrected; PostgreSQL restarted; archiving caught up overnight (74% on 7 Oct, 61% on 8 Oct).
