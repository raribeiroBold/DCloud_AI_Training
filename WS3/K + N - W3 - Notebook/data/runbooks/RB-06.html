Disk full on /var

Symptoms

Services stop writing logs or data; PostgreSQL stops with 'No space left on device'; the disk-check script logs 'WARN filesystem /var 97% used' and the monitoring alert 'filesystem /var above 95%' fires.

Procedure

  1. Find what grows: sudo du -xh /var | sort -h | tail -20
  2. Compress or remove rotated logs older than 7 days in /var/log.
  3. On database hosts, never delete files in pg_wal: follow RB-02.
  4. If /var is still above 90%, extend the logical volume and open a ticket for team-platform.
